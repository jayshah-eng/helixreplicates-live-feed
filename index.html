A report-derived ranking of estimated power for each study's primary claim. Power describes sensitivity at the published effect, not replication success.

Read the evidence in context

Direction agreement shows how many tested effects pointed the same way as the paper. The adjacent count separates those from findings that passed the report's significance threshold.

Power is estimated at the published effect size. These are pilot readouts, not final verdicts; the supplied reports say the verdict rule was set after results were seen.
